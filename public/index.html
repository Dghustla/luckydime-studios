Straight answers

The questions worth asking.

Who is Lucky Dime for?

People with a small, specific, annoying problem and no patience for software that makes it bigger. Collectors. Sellers. Growers. Hobbyists. If you have ever said "I just want the thing to do the thing," you are exactly who we build for.

Who is it not for?

Anyone shopping for dashboards, forty integrations, and a quarterly business review. There are big companies that live for that. Use one of them, and good luck. No hard feelings, and we will still be here when the job is not done.

Why trust a one-human studio?

Because you never have to trust us. You can test the work before you spend a dime. Spellstash is live right now, free to try, no credit card. Judge the craft yourself. The AI crew means the building never sleeps. The one human means somebody real answers when something breaks.

What is coming next?
